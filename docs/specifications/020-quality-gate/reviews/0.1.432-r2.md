### B1 resolved: cancellation owns the prepare process group

**Locations:** `src/core/code/gate/prepare.ts:262`, `:279`, `:284`; signal forwarding remains in `src/cli/code-gate-relaunch.ts:117`.

**Contract:** amended D15.3: “When the gate is stopped (SIGINT, SIGTERM), it stops the command's whole process group first.” D3.4 retains one budget.

`runCommand` installs its cancellation listeners while the command runs. It kills the detached group, polls for its end within the 1,000 ms close grace, removes its listeners, and raises the original signal again. Settlement removes the listeners and timer too. The cancellation promise prevents settlement from preceding the raised signal.

Repeated the previous review's bundled-CLI reproduction with `timeoutSeconds: 0.5` and `--timeout 2`. Extended the prepare script to spawn an ordinary descendant in its group. Both processes write their PIDs, append a heartbeat, and schedule a delayed write at 1,500 ms. Send the signal after both PID files exist. Inspect both processes at CLI close, then inspect heartbeat sizes and delayed markers after another 1,700 ms.

| CLI mode | Signal sent | CLI result | Signal to close | Live prepare/descendant at close | Writes after close |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| text | SIGINT | ended on SIGINT | 18 ms | none | none |
| text | SIGTERM | ended on SIGTERM | 18 ms | none | none |
| JSON | SIGINT | exit 3 | 28 ms | none | none |
