## Drop candidates

### 1. `leftover-temp` and `squeal ps --leftovers`

**What it is.** D7's `leftover-temp` lists temp directories of the names Squeal makes that no live daemon's key names, that no process uses and that are older than 1 h. `--leftovers` prints them for the end command `rm -rf` (D1, D7). Its code is `src/core/ps/flags-leftovers.ts` (333 lines); the source reads `cwds` and `unixSockets` (`src/core/ps/source.ts:138`, `:150`), which nothing else calls, and the open-file walk over every process that only it makes (`flags-leftovers.ts:236-242`; `discover.ts:160` reads one daemon's fds); the macOS `lsof` spawn (`darwin.ts:136-154`); the `--leftovers` path of the CLI (`src/cli/ps.ts:100-106`, `:115-117`); `PsUnknown.paths` (`shape.ts:270-271`); and the tests `flags-files-walk.test.ts` (183 lines), `flags-files-withheld.test.ts` (87), most of `fixture-host-rules.ts` (115), and the leftover cases of `flags-files.test.ts`, `snapshot-flags.test.ts`, `test/cli/ps.test.ts` and `test/e2e/ps.test.ts`.

**Vision clause: the decision filter.** Leftover temp directories are disk use, not validation state. The rule makes no feedback faster, more relevant or more trustworthy (1). It cuts no noise or blocking (2). It carries no provenance (3). It is no harness boundary (4). No project policy governs it (5). It is no transition and no state of a check (6). Principle 8 ("resource limits ... are all visible") is served by the daemons and test processes `ps` lists and by the other 17 flags.

**Evidence that it does not earn its cost:**

- It never lists a directory on this host. `status.md`'s shipped line accepts that it withholds every candidate while a process it cannot read is up, and on this host an SSH session always is. Round 3 withheld 335 to 354 directories per sample and listed none (`lessons.md` "P1, P5 and P8 live").
- After the store reads, it is the largest part of a snapshot. In Round 4's profiled run (681 processes, 33 daemons) it took 54 of 272 ms of busy samples: 34 ms in the use reads, 26 ms of them the open files, and 20 ms listing `/tmp` (`lessons.md` Round 4, the profile table). Goal 6's median was 0.33 s against 0.4 s.
- What it finds is mostly the debris of Squeal's own test suite on Squeal's own development host: key directories of test repositories (spec, Open questions, inferred there), e2e fixtures and test helpers' directories (`lessons.md` P11). A user's daemon reuses its worktree's key, and `prepareScratch` removes what an earlier daemon of that key left (`src/core/daemon/scratch.ts:96-124`).
- It took three dogfood rows (014-22, 014-23, 014-25) and two review blockers (`reviews/wave-2.md` B2, `reviews/wave-4.md` B2), and 014-36 is still open on it.

**What stays.** `stale-socket` and `stale-pool-mark` in the files section: they name files of daemons that died. The prevention goes where the directories are made: row 014-36 below for the test suite, and the 011 Later item for the e2e fixtures (`docs/board.md`, the e2e suite's `/tmp/squeal-e2e-*` directories, 84 of them and 6.3 GiB on 2026-10-21).

**Against the drop.** The human named temp directories in the Problem section and goal 4 (2026-10-21), before Rounds 2 to 4 measured the rule. The shape can stay `v: 1`: `leftover-temp` and `paths` would no longer be produced, which no reader can misread. D1, D7, D8 and goal 4 are amended. If the rule stays, row 014-36 still applies, and slice 8 rewrites the comment at `flags-leftovers.ts:33`.
