## Decisions the spec did not settle

- **W1's selection comes from the commit daemon.** The CLI cannot tell which files with a result at the commit the change selects. The commit ask now tells them (`withResult`), by the same rule as the files it queues (named, selected, no key, `--all-missing`). An older daemon sends none: the working tree then runs only the queued files, as before.
- **The first call still cannot ask a group after its bound.** Asking past `askBy` would break the call's time budget (D7.3). The text says how to ask it instead, as the brief asks.
- **A join's "idle" line** counts files with a result on one side and none on the other, nothing queued or running there; the commit side leaves out files the change did not select. It names only the call that queues them (`--run`, same paths).
- **W2's limit is over the whole call (20 file lines), not per group.** Past it, each group gets one line with its count and three paths, as the unknown groups do without paths.
- **W2's `--wait` names only files that were running when the join began.** At the commit, that is the daemon's rows with a pending mark at the start (an ask still selecting has no rows yet). In the working tree, it is the daemon's answer at the start.
- **The floor (V2 a).** The brief's example said "fewer than 10 durations or under 20 %". Taken alone, "fewer than 10" makes a side of 5 files with 4 durations unknown. The rule is therefore: under 20 %, or fewer than 10 and under half; every file known is never too few. 1 of 1,338 and 2 of 10 are unknown; 12 of 40 is a guess.
