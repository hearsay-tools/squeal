## Decisions the spec did not settle

- **The estimate moved from stderr into the report.** It now speaks of what still runs after the wait, which the call knows only then; one place, also in JSON. `tellsEstimate` is gone: the line shows whenever something still runs.
- **Measured rate over stored durations.** Round 6's own numbers (90 to 157 results in 320 to 400 s) are this call's rate; it already holds the pool's share and the load. Its time includes the commit daemon's install and keying, so it errs long, never short.
- **Pool share, not slots.** A side's width is its `fairShares` share of `capacity.hostSlots`, as the pool divides it for backlog demand, with this call's two sides replacing their own marks. The background ceiling (021 D18) is not applied: narrow runs are named waits, outside it.
- **Narrowed `--wait` waits for its own files only.** Before, it waited for every kept ask's files (round 6: 1,338) up to its bound.
