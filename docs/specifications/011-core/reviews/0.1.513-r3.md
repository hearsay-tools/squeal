## What fits

1. **The rest rule: proven in source and focused tests.** `wakes.ts:165` creates and counts a piece before calling its work in that piece's async context. `waitFor` at line 188 changes only the live piece in that context; it cannot subtract another piece's count. Its settlement reaction is registered before the caller's awaited continuation, so the piece counts again before that continuation acts. Ordinary awaited reads remain counted. The timer enters `#piece.exit` at line 334, so a pass cannot suspend the piece that armed its timer. Mutex tasks still count independently through the count hook. The new isolation probe found no crossing of counts through a queued lock turn, runner callback or immediate.

2. **The added `event` and `pieces` waits are sound at their current call sites: proven by source inspection, supported by integration tests.** `scheduler.ts:1490` uses `event` only for the pump's next event. Events come from separately counted flights, the drain and tracked wake work, or from external calls. Timer passes themselves prevent rest while they run. `scheduler.ts:1196` uses `pieces` only for the pump's final waits on flight promises and the drain, all of which were started with `track`. The envelope stops counting while its constituent pieces retain their own counts. These two kinds do not add an uncounted scheduler operation. Keeping the pump counted through those waits would reopen B1. The closed list is a rule for these ownership handoffs, not permission to label an arbitrary read as an event or piece.

3. **B1 and the gather case remain fixed: proven by the public scheduler tests.** A held runner allows rest. When the runner returns, rest waits through recording and the last pump pass. On the virtual clock, a future slot gather allows rest and reports its wake; after advancing 25 ms, rest waits through records and the pump. `executeTier` enters the runner synchronously, so the flight's `run` wait does not hide an asynchronous scheduler preparation before the runner.

4. **B4 is fixed by independent ownership: proven by the gated-read integration test and source.** `record.ts:143` tracks the ended reads separately before starting the slot record. `slot-records.ts:86` suspends only the flight waiting for the gather. Rest stays pending while the gated warm-up read runs, then reports the future gather after release, then waits for record and pump completion after advancing. Reads still overlap the gather; the repair does not serialize them.
