## Added rule 4 and the deadline bound: accepted, verified

Rule 4 is explicit in C4 step 5 and the 2026-10-29 status amendment, attributed to coordinator request `8b69856d`. `rankToBeat` applies the existing minimum live rank from the promoted-only tier's start. Other tier kinds retain their first-30-second rule. This prevents a queued file of the same wait from cutting running files on a tie. It does not retain protection after that promotion expires.

The independent timer probe used two running files with earlier promotions expiring at 100 ms and 200 ms, and a queued file of a later wait. The tier stood `named` before expiry. At 100 ms it was checked once and retained protection from the second file. At 200 ms it was checked once more and yielded to the later file, still before 30 s. No timer remained; advancing another 10 s caused no more callbacks. The candidate's ordinary edit-tier control yields immediately to the same later file.

The new bound does not reuse an expired rank. `check` freezes one clock reading for the decision. `rankAt` accepts only deadlines strictly greater than that reading. At a deadline, the next check drops that rank or takes the next live rank. `boundOf` limits a waiting promoted-only tier to that deadline; after cancellation or no waiting work it removes the bound. Existing remainder, overrun and claim bounds retain their own progress rules.

Two more independent cases checked this seam. A tier standing on a 20 s remainder advanced through both earlier ranks, then cancelled for an edit at 200 ms instead of waiting for estimate plus 30 s. A clock that crossed both deadlines between decision and timer-arm caused one immediate fresh check, then a yield with no timer left. Closing a tier's yield tracker also removed its pending deadline timer. No new timer-spin path was found.
