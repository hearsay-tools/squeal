The changed tests use a stopped 5 s heartbeat. They cover expiry both with and without a shutdown mark, and timeout with the mark. Until then the old daemon can still answer ping and no replacement waits for its lock. Cost: recovery from a long shutdown of that legacy release waits for its exit. No such failure was found with the shutdown meta mark or the 0.1.121 note-only mark. This is an accepted deviation, not a remaining blocker under amended C9.
