## Should-fix

### S1 residual. Plausible, nonblocking: timeout can remove an active publisher's lock

**Location:** `src/core/runner-host/remote/package-manager.ts:128`, `:136` through `:146`; regression test at `test/runner-host/remote/package-manager.test.ts:137`.

After waiting 10 seconds, a contender unlinks the lock without checking its owner or age. If A is still removing a large broken cache, or is suspended after its `made()` check, B can remove A's live lock and enter the same critical section. A's unconditional final unlink can then remove B's lock. This can reopen the cache-removal race that S1 described. The timeout measures B's wait, not proof that A has died.

Source establishes the unconditional timeout removal. No controlled slow-owner interleaving was run, so the resulting publication failure remains **plausible**, not a blocker. The passing `Promise.all` test checks the final cache but does not hold an owner past expiry or force the check/remove boundary.

**Suggested repair scope:** one worker in this helper and its test. On contention timeout, fail with context instead of stealing an unproven stale lock, or use an ownership-safe lock with explicit dead-owner recovery and owner-checked release. Add a deterministic test that holds the first publisher while a contender reaches its deadline; prove that at most one publisher owns the removal/rename section. Keep unique part directories and winner reuse.
