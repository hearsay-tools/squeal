## Previously blocking findings

### B1. Proven repaired: the early CRAP bounds every completion of the current loader set

Locations: `src/core/code/crap/file.ts:114`, `src/core/code/crap/join.ts:218`.

The early path now passes `inHand` to the join's `bound` argument. The full path leaves it false. The early denominator counts all of the function's own source lines from the count, excluding lines strictly inside nested functions by the existing rule. A later transform's executable own lines are a subset of this fixed set. The early covered lines come only from confirmed current-blob records and are a subset of the covered lines after the missing records arrive. Per-record function matching and hit detection do not change when another record is added.

Let `L` be all own lines, `E` the full executable own lines, `C` the lines covered so far and `D` the full covered lines. Then `C` is a subset of `D`, and `E` is a subset of `L`. Thus `|C| / |L| <= |D| / |E|`. The existing empty-executable case also preserves the inequality. CRAP decreases as coverage increases, including its 95% cutoff. The early score therefore cannot be below the full score for the same blob, counter and fixed records already in hand. Completing that loader set cannot re-fail a function that passed early.

The independent probe also checks a real multiline early pass at threshold 80, rather than only retaining the old failure at threshold 30. The candidate's `early-bound.test.ts` adds the original transform regression and preserves the simple early-pass case. A confirmed hit still resolves `uncovered` early. Full judgement retains the executable-line denominator and existing text. The accepted cost is fewer early CRAP passes when comments, blank lines or braces enlarge the conservative denominator.

### B2. Proven repaired: the early threshold guard applies during quiet recounts

Location: `src/core/code/crap/judge.ts:112`.

`kindOf` now decides the crossing before quiet suppression. An early judgement requires `crossing === "fail-to-pass"`. This preserves the check against the failure's stored threshold whether or not the source is quiet. Only after eligibility is established does `quiet` suppress writing the transition. The old quiet bypass is gone.

The candidate regression and the independent original reproduction both require `big fail 35` to remain while another loader is pending at threshold 40. Both require the ordinary quiet full judgement to write `pass 35` without a transition when the last record arrives.
