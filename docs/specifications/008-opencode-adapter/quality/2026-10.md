## Verdict

No code in the OpenCode adapter is dead, and no feature fails the vision's filter. Every hook, the bridge, the idle wake, the store at `dispose`, the status line and `install.sh --opencode` traces to a goal of the spec or to a proven review finding. The adapter's costs fall into three groups:

- **`turns.ts` grew by repair.** Repairs 008-16, 008-25, 008-28, 008-31 and 008-33 added a field each. The `Turn` record now has two counters of the same events (`epoch` and `arrivals`), and two flags (`continued`, `aborted`) that encode one three-way choice. That is finding 1.
- **OpenCode's copies of shared things that 009's scan did not name.** 009's file (`../../009-pi-adapter/quality/2026-10.md`, now row 009-29) counted four CLI-command formatters and two idle-wait bounds. OpenCode adds a fifth formatter, a third wait bound, a third unregistered-session check, and a cross-import of Pi's install text.
- **Constants the Bun bundle cannot import, restated without a pin.** One of them has the same name as a shared constant but a different meaning. The e2e diagnostics restate the plugin's deadlines as well.

`status.md`'s amendments match the code. The spec's own D4 and D6 do not: they still name one bridge entry, `dist/hooks/opencode.mjs`, which 008-12 replaced with one entry per request kind, and no amendment records the change (slice 6).

Counts: 0 drop candidates, 2 rows, 9 slices.
